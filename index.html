ABOUT THIS LIVING ARCHIVE

People before dates.

This student concept follows the supplied South-View / Praise House Project white paper. All twenty people come from the foundation’s neighborhood page. Every story starts with an individual, then opens into personal milestones and the history around their life.

Reading the connections

Documented event connection identifies a supported role or experience. Historical or thematic context explains a wider connection and does not imply that someone was present at an event. The Juneteenth document concerns Texas; the Atlanta emancipation stories are distinguished from it. The Child Murders chapters for Young and Yancey are explicitly thematic.

Images and voices

Portraits are credited to the providers named in South-View’s archive. Historical images have their own captions and source links. Original symbolic visuals are labeled and are not presented as archival evidence. No personal testimony or family anecdotes have been invented.

Continue the archive

Victims’ full life stories, interviews, and additional event connections require further research and community input. This is a private demonstration, not an official foundation publication. The directory reflects people featured by the foundation and makes no claim about current grave locations.
